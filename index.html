Feb 2024Research

Research Assistant · Lindsay Lab

Working closely with Professor Grace Lindsay and my coworker Chris Kong, I helped build a pipeline that pulls together more than five years of beaver dam locations and tracks how the environment around each dam changes over time, using measures like evapotranspiration (ET) and vegetation greenness (NDVI). Together, we built the analysis and a Streamlit app on top of it where anyone can upload new beaver dam files, run them through the same workflow, and explore visualizations of the changes around each dam.

What I love most about this project is that it turns environmental research into a tool people can keep using, to study how beaver dams may help landscapes become more resilient to floods and wildfires.

A big thank you to Professor Grace Lindsay for welcoming me into her lab, guiding me through my first real research project, and showing me how data science can help answer meaningful environmental questions.
